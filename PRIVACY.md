# Privacy

Ember stores your Venice key and chats in this browser. It does not save them in an Ember database.

Requests still leave the device. The key, prompts, transcripts, microphone audio, and text that will be spoken are sent through the app proxy to Venice. Voice changer uploads the audio file you queue.

There is no Ember product-analytics client.

Details, including storage keys and what is not stored, are in [docs/PRIVACY_MODEL.md](docs/PRIVACY_MODEL.md).
